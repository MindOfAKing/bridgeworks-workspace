# BridgeWorks content style

## Voice

- Use short sentences.
- Keep one main idea per sentence.
- Prefer specific facts, real numbers, dates, names, and sources.
- Write toward truth, not toward a more compelling claim.
- Sound human. Do not imply a team where one does not exist.
- Do not use em dashes.

## Capitalisation and punctuation

Use `BridgeWorks` with that capitalisation. Use sentence case for headings unless an established surface requires another form. Prefer periods, commas, colons, or parentheses over em dashes.

Serial-comma preference and title-case exceptions are unresolved.

## CTA style

Use one specific, low-friction next action. State what the reader should do and what happens next. Do not use false urgency, unsupported scarcity, or vague calls such as "unlock your potential".

## Citation and evidence

- Use only verified claims.
- Attach a source to dates, metrics, client outcomes, and public claims.
- Distinguish publication date from event date.
- Preserve uncertainty and conflicting evidence.
- Never fabricate testimonials, results, client approval, or volume claims.
- CEEFM public claims must follow `clients/ceefm/VERIFIED-FACTS.md`.
- Oliviks public name use is not approved unless newer evidence says otherwise.

## Public positioning

Describe BridgeWorks as an AI consultancy for founder-led SMEs. It is full-service, not an audit-only wedge. It is based in Budapest, focused on Central and Eastern Europe, and also serves Nigeria and Africa-Europe corridor businesses. Public service-route names remain unresolved until approved.

## Client separation

Use the client's approved brand and voice inside client-owned outputs. Keep BridgeWorks authorship, credits, and report framing separate. A "Site by BridgeWorks" footer appears only where contractually agreed.
