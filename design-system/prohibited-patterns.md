# Prohibited patterns

## Writing

- Em dashes.
- Unsupported claims, testimonials, numbers, dates, approvals, prices, or commitments.
- Copy that implies a large BridgeWorks team.
- CEEFM described as active or as a completed 16-week engagement.
- Client-sensitive findings in public copy.
- The phrases: world-class, innovative, seamless, ecosystem, game-changing, transformative, robust, cutting-edge, passionate about, leverage, synergize.
- Generic filler such as "in today's fast-paced world".

Do not rewrite third-party quotations, source titles, citations, code, URLs, or legally exact text merely to satisfy style lint.

## Visual

- Plain white as the default BridgeWorks background.
- Gold used as a full background.
- Playfair Display for body text or controls.
- Generic logos or icon sets presented as BridgeWorks identity.
- Stretched, recoloured, cropped, or improvised BridgeWorks logo variants.
- BridgeWorks tokens applied to a client brand without approval.
- Oliviks, CEEFM, church, event, MindOfAKing, or personal-brand tokens leaking into BridgeWorks output.
- Motion without a reduced-motion path.
- Informative images without alt text.
