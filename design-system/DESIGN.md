# BridgeWorks design rules

## Purpose

BridgeWorks is an AI consultancy for founder-led SMEs. It helps SMEs move from fragmented growth and manual operations to clearer strategy, stronger digital presence, AI-enabled systems, and disciplined execution.

The visual system should feel architectural, human, specific, and credible. It must not imply a large team or corporate scale.

## Colour

- Navy `#0F1A2E`: primary dark ground, power moments, hero and closing sections.
- Charcoal `#1C2B3A`: primary text and dark panels.
- Charcoal light `#243347`: verified live-site supporting surface.
- Charcoal lighter `#2D3F52`: verified live-site supporting surface.
- Gold `#B8860B`: the most important accent. Do not use as a full background.
- Gold light `#D4A843`: supporting gold.
- Ivory `#F5F0E8`: default light background. Do not substitute plain white.
- Sage `#4A6741`: secondary accent.
- Warm gray `#6B6560`: muted text, captions, and borders.

Use one ground per section. Prefer a navy opening, a long ivory middle, and a navy closer. Avoid patchwork alternating bands.

## Typography

- Body, UI and labels: Inter, weights 300, 400, 500 and 600.
- Headlines only: Playfair Display, weights 400 and 700.
- Code blocks in documents: Courier New.

Do not use Playfair for body copy or controls.

## Layout

- Use generous air and a restrained number of section patterns.
- Use one ground per section.
- Use the bridge arc motif sparingly as the signature visual.
- Use the arc-and-anchor-block vocabulary for custom icons.
- Avoid generic icon sets where a brand mark is required.

Spacing scale, content maximum width, grid columns, border radii, and standard component dimensions are unresolved.

## Logo treatment

Verified assets exist as light/dark horizontal logos and light/dark icon variants in the live agency repository. Use the matching contrast variant. Do not recolour, stretch, crop, or combine the BridgeWorks logo with a client logo into a new mark.

Minimum clear space, minimum rendered size, monochrome variants, and co-brand lockups are unresolved.

## Image direction

Use imagery that supports the real business, real people, specific work, and the bridge between contexts. Avoid visuals that imply a large team or unsupported corporate scale.

Photography treatment, illustration rules, texture, image aspect ratios, and generative-image policy are unresolved.

## Motion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Use restrained movement that clarifies hierarchy. Do not make motion necessary to understand content.

## Accessibility

- Provide meaningful alt text for informative images.
- Use empty alt text for decorative images.
- Preserve readable contrast and visible focus states.
- Do not encode meaning using colour alone.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

Target conformance level, minimum contrast exceptions, focus-ring tokens, and formal accessibility test tooling are unresolved.

## Mobile behaviour

Preserve reading order, tap-target clarity, short headings, and adequate spacing. Do not hide evidence, acceptance criteria, or required actions on mobile.

Exact breakpoints and responsive type/spacing scales are unresolved.

## Client separation

BridgeWorks tokens may frame a BridgeWorks-authored report, but client deliverables must use the approved client system. Never copy Oliviks, CEEFM, church, event, or personal-brand colours, fonts, logos, imagery, or tone into BridgeWorks assets.
