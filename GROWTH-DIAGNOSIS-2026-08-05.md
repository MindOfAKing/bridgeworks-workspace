## What each lens found

### Competitive research

The Africa-Europe corridor gap still holds and is the only genuinely unoccupied position found in either research pass.

Full detail: `research/competitive-bridgeworks-2026-08-05.md`

### Competitor intelligence

BridgeWorks is invisible. DR 0, no third-party citation anywhere, absent from every directory that ranks for Budapest agency searches.

Competitors in this set win through exactly three things: directory listings with reviews (Oxgital, Albanny), a free audit as lead magnet (Woww), and PR syndication (Nairobi Marketing). BridgeWorks does one of the three, badly, with no traffic reaching it.

At DR 0 with no directory presence, message refinement changes nothing. Nobody is reading the message.

### Market audit

The website is good. It is not the problem, and it should not be touched again this month.

Three real issues:

1. **Three competing CTAs.** "Get a Free GEO Audit", "Start the conversation", and newsletter subscribe all compete on the homepage. There is no single conversion action.
2. **No price anywhere.** Not on /services, not on /audit. The June research already flagged that hidden pricing is the norm competitors follow and transparent pricing was an available differentiator. It is still not being used.
3. **One case study, and it is the terminated client.** /work shows CEEFM only. It says "More case studies are coming."

**Oliviks is not on the website.** Paid in full, 272,611 HUF, Foundation delivery complete, site live and verified 2026-07-24, Restaurant Guru 2026 award. A completed, paid, happy engagement is sitting unpublished while the only public proof point is a client who terminated at week 9 of 16.

### Content

The 90-day rollout was approved 2026-07-24 with a formal launch on 2026-07-30. That date passed six days ago. There is no publishing receipt in `operations/content-system/receipts/`. The three receipts that exist are all audits of the content system itself, dated 2026-07-22.

The Week 1 authority queue was written on 2026-07-20 and has not been published.

The content is not blocked on quality. It is written. It is blocked on publishing.

### Ads

**Do not run ads.** Not this month, not next month.

Reasons, in order:

1. No validated offer. The entry product is free, so there is no conversion event with a value attached to it.
2. No price. You cannot compute an acceptable cost per acquisition without knowing what an acquisition is worth.
3. DR 0 and one case study. Paid traffic landing on thin proof converts badly and you will conclude the ads failed when the proof failed.
4. No baseline. There is no organic conversion rate to beat, so no way to tell a good campaign from a bad one.
5. Budget filter. Free first, low cost second, paid when value is proven. Nothing has been proven yet.

The gate for revisiting ads: three published case studies, a published price, and at least ten inbound enquiries from free channels so there is a conversion rate to optimise against. Until then ads buy traffic you cannot convert and cannot measure.
