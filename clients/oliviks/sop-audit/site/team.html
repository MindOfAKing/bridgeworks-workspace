Before you start

  • There is no name field. Answer honestly. Nobody is being checked up on and nobody is in trouble.
  • If the honest answer is "nobody ever told me", that is the most useful answer you can give. It means the fault is in the system, not in you.
  • If a question is not about your job, choose Not my job and move on.
  • Write in Hungarian if that is easier. We will translate it.
  • It takes about 10 minutes. Nothing leaves this phone or computer until you press Send.

Where your answers go

When you press Send, your answers go into a private file that only BridgeWorks can open. Aese and Cynthia cannot see it. They will be told what everybody said as a group, never who said what.

There is no name field on this form, and we record only the date you sent it, not the time, so it cannot be matched to who was on shift.

If you would rather not send it from your own phone, press Print this to hand in and put the paper in with the others.
