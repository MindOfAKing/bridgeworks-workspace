What this is for

An SOP is just a written way of doing one job, so the result is the same no matter who is on shift that day. How the egusi is portioned. Who checks the Wolt tablet. What happens when an order goes out with a missing item.

Right now most of that knowledge lives in your head and in Cynthia's. That works while you are both there. It stops working the day you want a holiday, a new hire, a second kitchen, or a bigger catering job.

This audit does not write the SOPs. It finds out where you need them most, so the work goes to the parts that are actually costing you money and time.

How to fill this in

  1. Answer honestly. "We just know how to do it" is a real answer and a very common one. There is no score to protect here.
  2. If a question does not apply to Oliviks, choose Does not apply and move on.
  3. You can stop and come back. Your answers stay in this browser on this device.
  4. At the end, press Send my answers. They go straight to BridgeWorks. If that fails for any reason, press Download and email the file to office@bridgeworks.agency.

Best filled by whoever actually runs the kitchen day to day. If you and Cynthia disagree on an answer, say so in the notes. That disagreement is useful information.
