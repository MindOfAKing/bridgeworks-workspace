# Arc Solutions Limited client audit

## Executive verdict

Arc has a credible, service-rich owned presence and a reachable quote path. The primary diagnostic issue is not the earlier suspected SPOTA placeholder. SPOTA is deliberately labelled **Coming Soon**. The stronger issue is owned-proof consistency: Arc publishes materially different project and client totals across its own pages, while other performance and certification claims lack visible substantiation on the sampled surfaces.

The primary route remains `digital-platforms-brand`. This is a trust, proof architecture and conversion-assurance problem. The audit did not establish a stronger GEO, internal workflow or operating-system deficit.

## Priority findings

| Priority | Finding | Evidence status | Commercial interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | Owned pages publish 500+ and 5,000+ project totals, plus 20+ corporate clients and 500+ satisfied clients | Verified contradiction on owned pages | A procurement buyer cannot know which scale claim is authoritative |
| Medium | ISO-certified-services claim lacks visible standard, scope, issuer and validity detail | Observed | Treat as a self-reported claim until Arc supplies the certificate context |
| Medium | 15-30% savings, 99%+ uptime and response claims lack visible methodology on the sampled service page | Observed | Do not repeat these as approved outcomes |
| Medium | No named decision maker or signing authority was established | Exhausted unknown | General contact remains viable, but named-buyer research should not loop without fresh evidence |
| Positive | General email, phones, locations and quote route are published | Verified | No missing-destination hard gate |

## Route confirmation

`digital-platforms-brand` is confirmed. The evidence supports a bounded owned-claims and procurement-trust reconciliation, potentially followed by a quote-route integrity review. `content-visibility-demand` remains secondary because no visibility deficit was established. `ai-workflow-automation` is not supported by the SPOTA teaser alone.

## Entry hypotheses

1. Reconcile project, client, coverage and certification claims into one approved proof source and apply it consistently across owned pages.
2. Verify the quote route from render through confirmation and internal ownership, but only with separate authorization because a submission would create contact.

## Limitations

No form was submitted. No authenticated analytics, CRM, Search Console or hosting data was used. Mobile and performance behavior were not measured in a browser lab. Client, scale, certification and outcome claims remain self-reported unless specifically marked verified.

## Stop point

Diagnostic complete. No outreach was prepared, no approval packet was created, and no CRM or scheduler state was changed.
