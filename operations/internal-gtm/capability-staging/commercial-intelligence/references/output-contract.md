# Commercial Intelligence output

Required fields: `capability`, `prospect_id`, `evidence`, `confidence`,
`commercial_value_band`, `unresolved_questions`, `research_status`, and
`external_action_authorized`.

`commercial_value_band` may be `low`, `medium`, `high`, or `unknown`. It describes
the supported economic significance of the problem, not a proposed fee.

`research_status` is `complete`, `partial`, `required`, or `not_applicable`.
