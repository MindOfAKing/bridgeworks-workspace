# Question Ladders

Ask no more than three questions per round. Skip anything already answered.

## Core Round

1. What is the one thought, event, or problem you want to say something about?
2. Who should recognize themselves in it?
3. What should they understand, decide, or do differently afterward?

## Story Round

Select only what is missing:

- What exact moment made this worth talking about?
- What was difficult, uncertain, tempting, or at risk?
- What did you first think, and what changed?
- What did you decide or do next?
- Which concrete detail can be public?
- What remains unresolved?
- Which words from your note must stay in your voice?

## Service Round

- Is this for someone starting from scratch, fixing a bottleneck, or trying to grow?
- Which gap is central: strategy, brand/platform, visibility/demand, automation, or execution capacity?
- What does the audience usually misunderstand about that gap?
- What would BridgeWorks actually do, in plain language?
- What defensible result or preferred future applies?
- What action should an interested reader take next?

## Article Round

- What is the claim or question the article must answer?
- Which example, counterexample, or objection gives it weight?
- What evidence is public and current?
- What is your view that research alone cannot supply?
- How much practical detail should the reader be able to use immediately?

## Format Round

- Which account is primary: Emmanuel, BridgeWorks, or another approved brand?
- Which output is needed now: article, post, carousel, Reel, Stories, Pin, newsletter, WhatsApp, or CMS entry?
- Is this a standalone piece or part of a larger narrative arc?

## Gap Rule

If Emmanuel says `draft with gaps`, do not keep interviewing. Draft with bracketed factual gaps and a separate verification list. Never turn an inference into a quote, story, metric, feeling, or result.
