# Output Contract

## Intake Return

1. Source note, transcript, link, or work event.
2. Direct Emmanuel statements.
3. Model inference, labeled separately.
4. Answered questions.
5. Up to three next questions.

## Story Brief

- Working title.
- Audience, buyer, moment, and problem.
- Starting, fixing, or growing route.
- Scene or source event.
- Tension and stakes.
- Decision or changed belief.
- Earned insight.
- Relevant BridgeWorks service.
- Evidence and source date.
- Privacy, consent, and claim status.
- Repetition check.
- Master format and CTA.

## Draft Return

1. Three hook options with different mechanics.
2. Recommended hook and why it fits the source.
3. Full master draft.
4. Claims and privacy review.
5. Channel adaptations requested or clearly useful.
6. Asset requirements.
7. Approval decisions.
8. Recommended next action.

## Status Values

- `captured`
- `questions-open`
- `brief-ready`
- `draft-ready`
- `revision-requested`
- `editorially-approved`
- `production-ready`
- `scheduled`
- `published`
- `measured`

Editorial approval does not authorize publishing. Publishing approval must name the exact piece, account, and intended time.
