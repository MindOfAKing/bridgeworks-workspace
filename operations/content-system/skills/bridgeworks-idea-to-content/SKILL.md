# BridgeWorks Idea to Content

## Overview

Turn incomplete founder material into story-led, service-relevant content without inventing experience or forcing a posting quota. Ask only the questions needed to make the source specific, then draft one canonical piece before adapting it.

## Required Context

Read the canonical local files when available:

- `C:\Users\User\Projects\BridgeWorks-Content-Studio\02_Strategy_And_Messaging\BRIDGEWORKS-CONTENT-OPERATING-SYSTEM.md`
- `C:\Users\User\Projects\BridgeWorks-Content-Studio\02_Strategy_And_Messaging\FOUNDER-MIND-OPERATING-SYSTEM.md`
- `C:\Users\User\Projects\BridgeWorks-Content-Studio\03_Inspiration_Research\EMMANUEL-VOICE-AND-THEME-CALIBRATION-2026-07-23.md`
- Recent briefs, drafts, approvals, and publishing history relevant to the topic.

The governing principle is: story leads, insight follows, evidence protects factual claims, and the offer arrives naturally.

## Choose the Intake Mode

- `quick-note`: a sentence, observation, link, screenshot, or mobile note.
- `work-to-story`: a current BridgeWorks build, diagnosis, decision, failure, recovery, or result.
- `voice-source`: a voice note or transcript.
- `finish-draft`: an incomplete article or post.
- `adapt-approved`: an approved master that needs native channel versions.

Do not ask which mode to use when the source makes it obvious.

## Interview in Small Rounds

Read `references/question-ladders.md`. Ask no more than three questions at once. Skip questions already answered by the source or canonical evidence. Use Emmanuel's reply to choose the next questions.

The first round must establish:

- What actually happened or what Emmanuel believes.
- Who needs this and what recognizable problem it addresses.
- The point, change, decision, or useful action the reader should leave with.

If specificity is still missing, ask for a scene, example, constraint, decision, or public evidence. Never invent personal details, feelings, dialogue, client facts, metrics, or outcomes.

If Emmanuel says `draft with gaps`, proceed and mark every unresolved item explicitly.

## Build the Canonical Story Brief

Before prose, produce:

- Source material and Emmanuel's direct words.
- Audience, buyer moment, and one specific problem.
- Story moment, tension, decision, and consequence.
- Earned insight and relevant BridgeWorks service route.
- Starting-from-scratch, fixing, or growth applicability.
- Evidence, privacy, consent, and repetition status.
- Intended master format and natural next action.

Keep direct statements separate from model inference.

## Draft One Master First

Draft the strongest canonical piece before creating derivatives. Default to an article or long LinkedIn post when the idea needs nuance. Use a shorter master when the idea is inherently visual or concise.

The draft must have:

- An honest hook grounded in the real tension.
- Concrete detail early.
- One clear argument.
- Plain-language expertise.
- Specific relevance to the intended audience.
- A natural BridgeWorks connection, not a pasted service catalogue.
- One proportionate CTA.

Avoid em dashes, unsupported certainty, generic motivational language, and rigid paragraph formulas.

## Adapt by Role, Not by Copying

After the master is editorially accepted, select only useful layers:

- Emmanuel LinkedIn: lived judgment, decision, and question.
- BridgeWorks LinkedIn/Facebook/Instagram: buyer pain, mechanism, service applicability, and next step.
- Article/newsletter: nuance, evidence, objections, and implementation.
- Carousel: one sequential diagnosis or framework.
- Reel/video: spoken hook, readable beats, visual actions, and approved BridgeWorks music direction.
- Stories: timely detail, question, progress, or proof fragment.
- Pinterest: evergreen search intent, native title and description, and a relevant owned destination.
- WhatsApp: concise founder introduction or useful update suitable for manual sharing.
- CMS/Sanity: approved article fields, excerpt, SEO title, description, CTA, and source ledger.

Personal and company versions must complement each other. Never duplicate captions across accounts.

Read `references/output-contract.md`.

- File durable briefs and review artifacts in the existing canonical Content Studio. Do not create a competing operating system or queue.
- End with the exact unanswered questions, approval decisions, and recommended next action.
