# Qualification Rubric

Use the current BridgeWorks ICP and offer canon when available. This rubric prevents stale geography, guessed pricing, and generic “free audit” logic from taking over.

## Scored Categories

Maximum: 100.

### Fit: 0-25

- 0: no credible need for a BridgeWorks service line
- 8: one relevant problem, but small or isolated
- 16: clear multi-function or commercially material problem
- 25: strong match to a defined BridgeWorks service route and likely implementation scope

### Active change: 0-25

- 0: no current motion found
- 8: weak or undated signal
- 16: one dated relevant event
- 25: multiple current signals or a time-bound initiative

Examples: funding, expansion, hiring for transformation/CRM/marketing/operations, new market entry, rebrand, platform migration, grant/voucher, tender, leadership mandate.

### Problem evidence: 0-20

- 0: only a generic industry assumption
- 7: one observable symptom
- 14: multiple connected symptoms
- 20: symptoms connect to a plausible commercial or operating consequence

### Capacity: 0-15

- 0: evidence suggests inability to absorb implementation
- 5: unknown
- 10: plausible operating capacity
- 15: clear investment or delivery capacity

Do not infer capacity from country, brand polish, or a famous founder.

### Buyer access: 0-15

- 0: no lawful, relevant route to a buyer
- 5: generic company channel only
- 10: named likely buyer or warm path
- 15: verified decision maker, referrer, reply, or inbound request

## Five Service Routes

1. `strategy-transformation`: unclear growth direction, market entry, transformation design, offer or operating model.
2. `digital-platforms-brand`: website/platform weakness, customer journey, conversion architecture, brand system.
3. `content-visibility-demand`: search/GEO, demand capture, content system, campaign and channel performance.
4. `ai-workflow-automation`: repetitive work, fragmented tools, AI readiness, workflow automation, data handoffs.
5. `execution-operating-systems`: CRM/RevOps, ownership gaps, delivery governance, reporting, cross-team execution.

Choose the route supported by the strongest commercial evidence, not the most visible cosmetic flaw.

## Exclusions

Reject or escalate for review when:

- the entity or opportunity is not legitimate;
- the requested outcome is unlawful, deceptive, exploitative, or outside BridgeWorks' services;
- there is no plausible decision-maker path;
- the likely engagement is too small to justify bespoke work and no repeatable campaign route exists;
- the company cannot reasonably implement the recommended outcome;
- the lead is a supplier, job seeker, competitor, or irrelevant solicitation rather than a buyer.

## Signal Count

A verified signal must be source-backed and decision-relevant. Count at most one signal for each duplicated claim across pages. The four-signal `audit-approved` threshold should normally span at least three of: fit, change, problem, capacity, access.
