# BridgeWorks Revenue System Scan

Identify one commercially meaningful revenue-system hypothesis without pretending to audit a CRM or internal process that BridgeWorks cannot see.

## Entry Gate

Require:

- `audit-approved` or `discovery-ready`;
- `execution-operating-systems` as the primary route;
- at least two recent, relevant first-party signals;
- one plausible executive owner.

If the gate fails, return to `bridgeworks-prospect-router`. For an explicitly requested forward test, label the result `internal-test-draft` and do not release it.

## Timebox

1. Three minutes: confirm offer, buyer, commercial motion, and trigger.
2. Six minutes: verify public conversion, hiring, leadership, or operating evidence.
3. Three minutes: form one primary revenue-system hypothesis.
4. Three minutes: choose three actions.
5. Three minutes: show one future-state map.
6. Two minutes: cite, qualify, and inspect.

Stop at 20 minutes. If fewer than two useful first-party signals exist by minute nine, do not issue the asset.

## Evidence Classes

Label each material statement:

- `verified`: directly observed, dated, and linked;
- `hypothesis`: plausible interpretation to test;
- `unknown`: requires internal evidence.

A prospect-ready asset requires a URL and observation/publication date for every `verified` signal. If either is missing, label the result `internal-test-draft` and block release.

Use confidence consistently:

- `high`: direct, current first-party evidence;
- `medium`: current evidence with one material interpretation;
- `low`: indirect, stale, or incomplete evidence. Low-confidence evidence cannot carry the primary hypothesis.

Technology detection proves presence at most. It does not prove configuration, integration, adoption, governance, data quality, or performance.

Useful public evidence includes:

- high-intent pages, calls to action, forms, confirmation and scheduling paths;
- pricing, packaging, qualification promises, and buyer routing;
- customer cases and implementation claims;
- current Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, Operations, CRM, or RevOps jobs;
- leadership changes, acquisitions, expansion, and transformation announcements;
- named executive ownership and operating cadence statements.

Do not call an inferred condition a gap, leak, failure, bottleneck, or root cause.

Choose the buyer who owns the commercial consequence and decision rights. A vacancy is a change signal, not a contact. If the accountable role is open, route to the current executive owner and name the incoming role only as a future owner.

## Diagnostic Logic

Form one sentence:

`Because we observed [verified signals], we hypothesize [constraint or decision], which may affect [commercial consequence].`

Add:

> This is a public-evidence hypothesis, not an internal finding. CRM configuration, conversion, ownership, process, and performance data were not reviewed.

Use one alternative hypothesis only when it could materially change the recommended next step.

## Output

Follow [references/one-page-template.md](references/one-page-template.md). Keep the asset under 600 words.

Produce:

- `REVENUE-SYSTEM-SCAN-[company]-[date].md`;
- a branded PDF when visual hierarchy improves comprehension.

Use the active PDF/document workflow and inspect the final artifact. Do not use `market-report-pdf`.

## Recommendations

Return exactly three actions:

1. one immediate clarification or decision;
2. one validation or measurement action;
3. one operating action.

For each, name the likely owner, intended outcome, and first success indicator. These are recommendations, not claims that the prospect lacks them.

## Future-State Demonstration

Show only the narrow slice supported by public evidence:

`high-intent entry -> qualification -> accountable owner and response rule -> accepted/nurture/disqualified disposition -> recorded outcome -> exception review`

Adapt the fields and stages to the prospect. Do not design a full CRM schema, dashboard, automation, or SOP.

## Truth-System Handoff

For BridgeWorks execution:

- HubSpot stores company, contact, qualification, stage, touch history, and next action.
- ClickUp stores research, production, QA, approval, and follow-up tasks.

Prepare payloads only. Confirm connection and approval before external writes.

## Stop and Paid Boundary

Stop when:

- the prospect is not qualified or no current trigger exists;
- fewer than two first-party signals can be verified;
- the commercial motion is not publicly legible;
- the only observations are generic website-copy critiques;
- the hypothesis requires internal data to state responsibly;
- the evidence is stale or an earlier diagnostic has no material new trigger.

The free scan identifies where to investigate. A paid audit may access CRM, automation, analytics, calls, email, finance, and customer data; validate lifecycle, routing, ownership, SLAs, integrations, governance, and data quality; quantify commercial impact; and design the operating model and implementation plan.

Never include pricing, speculative ROI, a portal audit, a full operating model, a proposal, or a claim about staff competence.

Before release, confirm:

- one hypothesis and at most one alternative;
- three recommendations only;
- one lifecycle slice only;
- no invented metric, dashboard, schema, automation, or SOP;
- all verified signals have URLs and dates;
- every internal condition remains a hypothesis or unknown;
- the buyer owns the stated consequence.
