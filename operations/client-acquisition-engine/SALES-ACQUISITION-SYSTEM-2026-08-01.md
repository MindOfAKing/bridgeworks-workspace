# BridgeWorks Sales Acquisition System

Date: 2026-08-01  
Owner: Emmanuel Ehigbai  
Status: Internal operating build; external actions remain approval-gated  
Scope: All five BridgeWorks service routes

## Commercial decision

BridgeWorks should not run five unrelated outbound campaigns. It should run one account-based commercial system with five diagnostic entry routes:

1. Direction — Strategy & Transformation.
2. Credibility — Digital Platforms & Brand Systems.
3. Demand — Content, Visibility & Demand.
4. Leverage — AI & Workflow Automation.
5. Delivery — Execution & Operating Systems.

The seller's job is to identify the prospect's most expensive visible constraint, start with the corresponding diagnostic, and expand only after discovery proves a connected need.

## What the best sellers do

The research supports seven operating principles.

### 1. Win before the first conversation

6sense surveyed 2,509 recent B2B buyers and reported that 81% had selected a preferred vendor before speaking with sales and 85% had largely established their requirements. BridgeWorks therefore needs recognizable, useful ideas in market before asking for meetings.

BridgeWorks adaptation:

- Emmanuel LinkedIn carries founder judgment and practical insight.
- BridgeWorks LinkedIn and the website hold institutional proof, methods, service pages, and case evidence.
- Outreach references one useful diagnostic asset rather than asking for a generic introduction call.

Source: https://6sense.com/science-of-b2b/2024-buyer-experience-report/

### 2. Become a source of advice, not a supplier asking for attention

RAIN Group's research of more than 1,000 sellers and managers found top performers were 88% more likely to inspire buyers to reach out for advice and ideas. They also outperformed in needs discovery, value cases, relationships, and action planning.

BridgeWorks adaptation:

- Publish decision rules, teardown lessons, checklists, and before/after operating diagrams.
- Make the first commercial interaction a useful diagnosis.
- Ask for a small next step: permission to send observations, compare two routes, or map one workflow.

Sources:

- https://www.rainsalestraining.com/sales-research/the-top-performing-seller
- https://www.rainsalestraining.com/sales-training-company/news/the-top-performing-seller-research-report

### 3. Personalize around business relevance

Gong analyzed more than 30,000 prospecting emails from more than 250 companies and distinguishes individual-, company-, activity-, and industry-based personalization. For BridgeWorks, company- and activity-based relevance should lead; personal trivia should not.

BridgeWorks adaptation:

- One current observable signal.
- One plausible consequence, clearly framed as a hypothesis.
- One service-relevant insight.
- One low-pressure next step.
- No generic compliment, invented metric, or unsupported claim.

Source: https://www.gong.io/blog/4-data-backed-ways-to-increase-your-email-reply-rate-and-book-that-meeting

### 4. Use thought leadership as sales infrastructure

The 2024 Edelman–LinkedIn research reports that 75% of decision-makers and C-suite executives researched a product or service they had not previously considered after encountering a piece of thought leadership, and nine in ten were more receptive to outreach from companies producing consistently high-quality thought leadership.

BridgeWorks adaptation:

- Each service route gets a recurring diagnostic franchise.
- Each outbound batch is paired with one relevant public asset.
- Content uses research, a recognizable buyer problem, concrete guidance, and proof boundaries.

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/business/marketing/blog/research-and-insights/b2b-thought-leadership-research-impact-linkedin-edelman

### 5. Sell through several trusted paths

HubSpot's survey of 1,000 global sales professionals found social media was reported as producing the highest cold-outreach response rate by 42% of respondents, compared with 26% for email and 23% for phone.

BridgeWorks adaptation:

- Warm introduction first when a real relationship path exists.
- Founder-led LinkedIn visibility and meaningful engagement second.
- Evidence-backed email third.
- Referral request after value has been delivered.
- No automated multi-channel harassment.

Source: https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/hubspot-sales-strategy-report

### 6. Quantify value and run a real discovery

RAIN Group reports that top performers were 63% more likely to excel at communicating ROI and financial cases, 58% more likely to lead thorough needs discoveries, and 60% more likely to present persuasive overall value cases.

BridgeWorks adaptation:

- Discovery establishes current cost, delay, risk, ownership, decision process, and success measure.
- Proposals show the cost of the current state, the target operating change, evidence boundaries, and an adoption plan.
- No proposal is produced from a superficial website observation alone.

### 7. Automate preparation, preserve human judgment

Salesforce's survey of 5,500 sales professionals found reps reported spending 70% of their time on non-selling tasks. Automation should remove research reconciliation, record maintenance, reminders, and reporting—not automate trust or send unreviewed claims.

BridgeWorks adaptation:

- Composio coordinates read-only research and approved cross-app actions.
- HubSpot holds commercial truth.
- ClickUp holds work and due dates.
- Apollo is used only after ICP, HubSpot, Gmail, and credit-budget gates.
- Emmanuel approves exact external actions.

Source: https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/sales-ai-statistics-2024/

## The BridgeWorks commercial flywheel

### Stage 1 — Select

Build named-account pools by service route, buyer role, market, observable trigger, and likely business consequence.

Entry criteria:

- Real organization with credible demand evidence.
- Accountable buyer role can be identified.
- One current public trigger is sourced and dated.
- BridgeWorks has a relevant diagnostic or proof route.
- No exclusion, existing-client conflict, or prior-contact conflict.

### Stage 2 — Warm

For Tier A accounts:

- Follow the company and relevant buyer.
- Engage only where Emmanuel has a real contribution.
- Publish or reshare one service-specific diagnostic asset.
- Seek a warm introduction when a legitimate relationship path exists.

### Stage 3 — Diagnose

Use a small, specific entry offer:

| Service route | Diagnostic entry |
|---|---|
| Strategy & Transformation | Direction-to-Execution Brief |
| Digital Platforms & Brand Systems | Credibility and Conversion Review |
| Content, Visibility & Demand | Authority and Demand Gap Scan |
| AI & Workflow Automation | Workflow Friction Map |
| Execution & Operating Systems | Execution System Health Check |

### Stage 4 — Converse

The first CTA is permission-based:

- “Would it be useful if I sent the three observations?”
- “Worth comparing the two routes?”
- “Would a one-page workflow map help?”

The second CTA is a 20-to-25-minute diagnostic conversation only after interest.

### Stage 5 — Discover

Every discovery must establish:

- What is happening now?
- Where is value lost through delay, inconsistency, rework, weak trust, or unclear ownership?
- Why has the problem persisted?
- Who owns the outcome and who can block the decision?
- What would change in 30, 60, and 90 days?
- What evidence would make the engagement successful?

### Stage 6 — Prove and propose

Match only the proof BridgeWorks can safely use. Present:

- current-state problem;
- target outcome;
- intervention and boundaries;
- adoption owner;
- quantified or bounded value case;
- phased commercial option;
- next mutual action.

### Stage 7 — Expand and refer

At a verified value milestone:

- ask what connected constraint is now visible;
- ask whether another team or peer faces the same problem;
- request a specific introduction;
- offer a reusable insight or artifact the client can forward.

## Weekly operating cadence

| Day | Commercial work | Target |
|---|---|---:|
| Monday | Select and reverify accounts | 20 |
| Tuesday | Research buyers, triggers, and relationship paths | 10 |
| Wednesday | Publish one authority asset and prepare five exact approaches | 5 |
| Thursday | Review replies, run discovery, prepare approved actions | 100% due |
| Friday | Pipeline review, value cases, referral opportunities, learning | 1 review |

Initial quality target:

- 25 active accounts: five per service route.
- 10 Tier A accounts receiving manual research.
- Five approval-ready approaches per week.
- No volume target overrides evidence quality, suppression, or legal review.

## Pipeline definitions

| Stage | Exit condition |
|---|---|
| Target | ICP fit and source recorded |
| Researched | Buyer, trigger, evidence, and service route recorded |
| Warmed | Relevant engagement, introduction path, or matched content asset recorded |
| Approach ready | Exact destination, copy, evidence, and approval state recorded |
| Contacted | Verified external event exists |
| Engaged | Human reply or meaningful conversation exists |
| Discovery | Problem, owner, consequence, and decision path established |
| Qualified opportunity | Real initiative, fit, buyer access, next action, and plausible commercial value |
| Proposal | Reviewed scope and mutual action plan delivered |
| Won / Lost / Nurture | Reason and learning recorded |

## Guardrails

- No send, publish, form submission, paid enrichment, CRM write, or ClickUp task creation without the applicable approval.
- Do not imply an internal operational problem from a public website signal; use the signal as a discovery hypothesis.
- Do not create a HubSpot deal until a real commercial opportunity exists.
- Do not use Apollo before exact target count and credit budget approval.
- Honor suppression, objections, and applicable privacy/ePrivacy requirements.
