# Appinio Revenue System Decision Snapshot
## What to keep stable, validate, and operationalize during the new-CEO and Senior RevOps transition

**Public-evidence diagnostic**

**Prepared for:** Arne Wolter, CEO

**Review date:** 1 August 2026

**Prepared by:** BridgeWorks

## Why this question is timely

Appinio appointed Arne Wolter in May 2026 to lead its next growth phase. The same first-party release describes a business operating in more than 190 markets, with about 250 employees and more than 3,000 customers.

Appinio's live Greenhouse board also lists a Senior Revenue Operations Manager. The role owns work across Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success, including HubSpot, workflow design, the data model, automated data-quality controls, CRM health reporting, and operating documentation.

## Working hypothesis

Because Appinio has a new growth mandate and is recruiting a senior operator across its revenue workflow and data model, the immediate opportunity is to separate three layers of ownership: what the CEO keeps stable now, what Business Intelligence validates, and what the Senior RevOps hire operationalizes.

> This is a public-evidence hypothesis, not an internal finding. BridgeWorks did not review Appinio's HubSpot configuration, conversion, ownership, process, or performance data.

An alternative explanation is that the role adds capacity to an already well-governed system. The first step should test decision rights and one lifecycle slice, not assume a deficiency.

## Three decisions worth taking

1. **Keep stable:** Approve a one-page revenue decision charter covering the priority lifecycle, executive measures, and who resolves cross-functional exceptions.  
   **Owner:** CEO  
   **First indicator:** one approved owner for each decision class.

2. **Validate:** Inspect one high-intent journey from qualified research request to accepted, nurtured, or disqualified outcome. Check owner, timestamp, reason, and next action.  
   **Owner:** Team Lead Business Intelligence  
   **First indicator:** a baseline for disposition completeness and unresolved exceptions.

3. **Operationalize:** Give the Senior RevOps hire a defined first-30-day mandate: confirm the lifecycle slice, fix recurring exceptions, and publish the operating definitions.  
   **Owner:** Senior RevOps Manager after hire  
   **First indicator:** recurring requests produce permanent controls, not isolated fixes.

## Five questions to test

1. Which cross-functional revenue decisions currently return to the CEO?
2. Which lifecycle slice matters most to the next growth phase?
3. Which HubSpot fields and dispositions are mandatory across teams?
4. Where do Marketing, Sales, Research, and Customer Success need the same definition?
5. What should the Senior RevOps hire be able to prove after 30 days?

## Scope boundary

This public review identifies where to investigate. A paid audit determines whether the issue is real, quantifies its commercial impact, locates the root cause, and designs the implementation plan.
