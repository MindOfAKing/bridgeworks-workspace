Working hypothesis
BridgeWorks can capture enquiries and now has reliable routing logic. We hypothesize that the constraint is the
missing control between stored contact and approved commercial action, leaving asset effort, ownership, and
learning disconnected.
This is an operating hypothesis. Current conversion performance, response
time, CRM configuration, and revenue attribution were not fully audited.
The manual process may work but lack real volume. Test five accounts before automating.
