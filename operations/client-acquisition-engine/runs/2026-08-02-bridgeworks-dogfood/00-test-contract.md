# BridgeWorks Prospecting Orchestration Dogfood Test

**Run date:** 2 August 2026  
**Run type:** Internal prospect simulation  
**Commercial owner:** Emmanuel Ehigbai  
**External actions:** None

## Test question

Can BridgeWorks take itself from observed buyer moment to qualification, one primary service route, one useful proof asset, and governed execution without creating duplicate commercial truth or defaulting to a generic audit?

## Pass conditions

1. BridgeWorks is scored using the active lead rubric with no insider exception.
2. One primary service route, commercial question, asset, and buyer are selected.
3. The chosen specialist scan follows its evidence and scope rules.
4. The asset contains company-specific evidence, exactly three actions, and one future-state demonstration.
5. HubSpot and ClickUp ownership rules are respected.
6. Unsupported claims, production changes, and external sends are excluded.
7. The run records what worked, what failed, and what should be implemented next.

## Evidence boundary

Public website observations are treated as public evidence. Local operating documents, connector checks, and ClickUp records are treated as internal operator evidence. Neither is used to infer revenue performance, conversion performance, or internal CRM health that was not directly inspected.
