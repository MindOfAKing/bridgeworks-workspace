## The three practical fixes

Connect the counter to a verified number and label, or remove it until the underlying data is ready.

Check the English and Hungarian versions after the correction. Also check mobile rendering and any cached page version.

Why it matters: this is one of the first proof signals an owner sees. Showing zero recommendations weakens confidence before the stronger evidence appears.

### 2. Give the performance figures a clear evidence frame

Keep the useful numbers, but add the period, population, and calculation method behind them.

Reconcile `within a few weeks` with the FAQ's `within one month`. If both are accurate, explain that one is a typical range and the other is the measured average.

Why it matters: precise numbers are persuasive when a visitor can understand what they measure and how the statements fit together.

### 3. Replace the unsupported price superlative with verifiable value

Replace `Best price in the Budapest market!` with a statement Homever can demonstrate.

Useful supporting detail already exists:

- the 8% management fee;
- multi-property discounts;
- the service scope;
- the 30-day satisfaction guarantee;
- the promised one-business-day contact response.

State any important guarantee exclusions or conditions beside the guarantee.

Why it matters: a transparent value case is more credible than an unqualified market-wide claim.
