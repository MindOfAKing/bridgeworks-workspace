## The three practical fixes

### 1. Correct the company name everywhere the shared footer appears

Change `Premier Propery Management Ltd.` to the verified legal or trading name.

Because the footer is likely reused across the site, check every Hungarian and English page after the correction. Also check page titles, structured data, legal pages, email templates, and downloadable documents for the same spelling.

Why it matters: the footer sits beside contact and legal information. A company-name error there creates avoidable doubt at the point where an owner is deciding whether the business is careful enough to manage a valuable property.

### 2. Replace the broad opening claim with visible proof

The homepage says Premier provides the best service, but it does not immediately show why a visitor should believe that.

Move concrete proof into the first decision section. Suitable existing material includes:

- named managing directors and specialists;
- the depth of the property-management knowledge library;
- the specific management, letting, finance, furnishing, and investment routes;
- one concise explanation of the management process;
- a clear route to the relevant decision-maker.

Avoid adding unsupported superlatives. Let the visible evidence carry the claim.

Why it matters: international owners often compare several firms quickly. Specific proof is easier to trust, remember, and verify than a general promise.

### 3. Build one clear owner enquiry path

Give an owner one short route from problem to action:

1. What Premier manages.
2. Who the service is for.
3. What happens after an enquiry.
4. What information the owner should prepare.
5. Which named person will respond.

Use one primary call to action across the relevant page instead of asking the visitor to infer the next step from several service and contact routes.

Why it matters: Premier already has substantial information. The opportunity is to reduce decision effort, not add more pages.
