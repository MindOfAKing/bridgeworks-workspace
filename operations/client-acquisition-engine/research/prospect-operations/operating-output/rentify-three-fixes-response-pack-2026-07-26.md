## Evidence boundary

Observed on `https://rentify.hu/en/` on 2026-07-26:

- The page states that empty properties cost landlords an average of 10% each year, tenants fail to pay in 1 in 7 Budapest properties, and landlords spend over two weeks each year dealing with property issues.
- No visible source or date accompanies those claims in the inspected section.
- Visible English includes `Proactivness`, `resolve all problem`, and `commited`.
- The page contains service detail, pricing, a portfolio, named leadership, online reporting, and a 24/7 support statement.

No claim is made here that the statistics are false. The issue is that a visitor cannot immediately verify their source, scope, or date.

## The three practical fixes

### 1. Source, qualify, or remove the landlord statistics

For every consequential statistic, add:

- the original source;
- the geography covered;
- the period measured;
- the publication date;
- a link that a visitor can verify.

If reliable evidence is unavailable, rewrite the section as a clearly framed business problem without numerical precision.

Why it matters: the page is asking owners to trust Rentify with an income-producing asset. Unsourced numbers can weaken otherwise strong operational claims.

### 2. Run a complete English buyer-path review

Correct the visible errors, including `Proactivness`, `resolve all problem`, and `commited`.

Do not stop with those three examples. Review the complete English route from the homepage through services, pricing, case studies, contact, confirmation messages, and any automated reply.

Check grammar, sentence structure, capitalization, duplicated ideas, incomplete phrases, and differences between the Hungarian and English offers.

Why it matters: Rentify sells careful management. The English communication should demonstrate the same attention to detail.

### 3. Put the strongest proof beside the decision

The page already contains useful proof and process information. Bring the most decision-relevant material together near the owner enquiry:

- the named team member responsible for the relationship;
- what the online reporting contains;
- the service and fee structure;
- one or two verifiable case examples;
- what happens after the owner requests an offer;
- the realistic response time.

Keep the 24/7 emergency-service statement separate from the sales-response promise so the two are not confused.

Why it matters: the current page contains plenty of material, but the visitor has to assemble the trust case from several sections.
