## Reconciled sales rules

- Geography is a filter. Buyer moment is the market.
- A broken page proves a problem, not buying intent.
- Generic local microbusiness outreach must not be scaled.
- Free work is earned by qualification.
- One prospect receives one primary route and one proof asset.
- Initial contact explains why the company was selected now.
- HubSpot is commercial truth. ClickUp is execution truth.
- Gmail sending, LinkedIn action, Apollo credits, and connector writes remain
  approval-gated.
