# Competitive Research: BridgeWorks (Delta Update)

**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Researched for:** BridgeWorks
**Supersedes:** `competitive-bridgeworks-2026-06-28.md` on the points below. Everything not contradicted here still stands.

## 1. What changed since 2026-06-28

### The CEE GEO window is closing

The June research recorded no AI-first or GEO positioning among Budapest agencies. That is no longer true. As of August 2026, Budapest agency listings describe firms combining classic SEO with AEO and GEO to place clients in Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT answers. Agencies serving KKV and B2B companies in Budapest are now marketing monthly AI-driven growth systems.

Implication: "first GEO agency in CEE" was never claimed publicly, and the window to claim it credibly is narrowing. The Nigeria gap recorded in June is not contradicted by anything found here, but it also was not re-verified.

### BridgeWorks is absent from every discovery surface

Searched the agency listings that rank for Budapest agency queries: TechBehemoths, Sortlist, Semrush Agency Partners, AI Superior, F6S, Clutch. BridgeWorks appears in none of them.

The only search footprint BridgeWorks has is its own website (`/en/about`, `/en/work`) and Emmanuel's LinkedIn profile. There is no third-party citation anywhere.

**Ahrefs Domain Rating: 0.0.**

This is the most consequential finding in this update. Competitors in this set win through third-party validation. Oxgital and Albanny Technologies both lead with Clutch listings and awards. Budapest agencies are listing-dependent. BridgeWorks has zero presence on any of them.

$99 is the documented floor for a paid one-off audit. A free audit sits below the floor of the cheapest paid product in the category.

## 2. What did not change

The June findings on the Africa-Europe corridor gap, the client-ownership gap, and the AI-built-but-documented gap were not contradicted by anything found in this pass. No competitor found in either pass occupies the corridor.

One thing strengthened the corridor thesis: Oliviks is a Nigerian-owned food business operating in Hungary, it paid in full, and delivery is complete. That is the corridor ICP with a completed paid engagement behind it. In June the corridor was a positioning argument. It now has one piece of evidence.

---

## 3. Recommendation change

June recommended three positioning angles. Two still hold. One needs demotion.

**Still holds, now with evidence: the Africa-Europe corridor.** Oliviks is proof, not theory. Use it.

**Still holds: client-owned outputs.** No competitor found in either pass makes this a central promise.

**Demote: "Nigeria's first dedicated GEO agency."** BridgeWorks has published nothing in Nigeria, has no Nigerian client, and has not tested demand there. Claiming a first-mover title in a market with no presence is a claim the evidence cannot support. Park it until there is a Nigerian engagement.

**Add: get cited by something other than yourself.** At DR 0 with zero directory presence, positioning is not the binding constraint. Discoverability is. No amount of message refinement moves a business that does not appear in the places buyers look.
